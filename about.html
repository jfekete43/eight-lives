ABOUT THE GRID

The Game Grid was born from a passion for both the history of gaming and the thrill of competitive arcade play. As a developer and enthusiast, I wanted to create a platform that honors the classic consoles of the past while providing a modern, high-stakes experience for players who love guessing games and trivia.

Development Philosophy

This project is built from the ground up to emphasize "game feel." Every decision, from the CRT-style scanlines and vignetting to the custom sound engine, is designed to recreate the feeling of standing in front of a real arcade cabinet. I believe that browser-based games should not feel like static web pages; they should be immersive, responsive, and challenging.

Technology Stack

The Game Grid is a modern web application hosted on GitHub Pages, utilizing Google Firebase for its backend infrastructure. This allows for real-time global leaderboards, secure user profiles, and persistent "Locker" customization, ensuring that your progress and items are saved across any device you play on.

Project Roadmap

This is a living, evolving project. My goal is to continuously expand our database of retro and modern titles, implement new genre-specific challenges, and improve the Item Shop to give players more ways to customize their profiles. Stay tuned for future updates, bug fixes, and seasonal challenge events that will test your knowledge of gaming history even further.
